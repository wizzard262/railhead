# Railhead: campaign ideas

Future campaigns for the Railhead engine. The rough map sketch for Normandy is [normandy-1944-sketch.html](normandy-1944-sketch.html) ([picture](normandy-1944-sketch.png)).

## Case Blue 1942 (least work)

German summer drive to Stalingrad and the Caucasus. Same direction, side and rules as 1941; mostly new data (map, lines, rivers Don and Volga, objectives, forces). Sets up a Stalingrad airlift later. About one session.

## Soviet defence 1941: trade space for time

Play the Red Army on the current map. Hold the Smolensk–Roslavl line until the deadline while making the German supply line fail. Tools: scorched earth (choose what to blow and when), evacuate engines and factories east, rearguards, prepared river lines, partisans and bombers on German railheads, counterattack timing (Yelnya), reserves arriving from the east. The German side runs on the existing engine as the opponent. About two sessions.

## Normandy 1944: the Allied supply race

- **Entry points instead of Warsaw:** beaches (big but closed by storms), Mulberry harbours (a storm can wreck one), captured ports that arrive wrecked and need clearing; Antwerp only works once the Scheldt is cleared; optional fuel pipeline
- **Rail repair instead of gauge conversion:** French track exists but bridges are bombed
- **Trucks are king:** Red Ball one-way routes; trucks wear out and need rest
- **Cargo:** fuel, ammo and rations; the pursuit is mostly fuel
- **Sectors:** British & Canadian (Caen → Antwerp), US First Army (Saint-Lô → Aachen), US Third Army (Avranches → Metz)
- **The big choice:** broad front or one thrust (Patton or Montgomery?)
- **Enemy and events:** bocage (high resistance, needs artillery), breakout, counterattacks on overstretched spearheads (Mortain), Channel storms, Allied air power, the German border stiffening in September
- **Win:** Paris, Antwerp working as a port, Aachen, Metz before the November rains; stars for speed and waste
- About 2–3 sessions plus balancing

## Napoleon 1812

Similar ground (Vitebsk, Smolensk, Orsha, Borisov and the Berezina, Minsk) but no railways: wagon trains, river barges, depots (magazines), foraging, horses dying as the main cost, then winter. A new map from the Niemen to Moscow. About 2–3 sessions.
