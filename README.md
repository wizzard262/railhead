# Railhead

Summer 1941. You run supply for Army Group Centre. The generals fight; you decide what reaches them.

Take Vitebsk, Smolensk and Roslavl by 4 August by keeping three sectors fed with fuel and ammo:

- **Trains** run only on converted track; Russian broad gauge stops them at the railhead
- **Trucks** carry the gap from railhead to front, carrying less and burning more as it grows
- **Engineers** convert track and repair stations the Soviets wrecked
- **Bombers** hit Soviet build-ups that air recon reports
- **Reserves** go east by train: infantry, panzers and artillery, each with its own appetite
- The computer-run Red Army counterattacks your weakest sector and bombs your fullest depot
