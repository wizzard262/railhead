    How to play

    Summer 1941. You run supply for Army Group Centre. The generals fight; you decide what reaches them. Take Vitebsk, Smolensk and Roslavl by 10 August.

    • 🚂 Trains run only on converted track. Russian track is a wider gauge, so your trains stop where the converted track ends: the railhead. That can be past the last station.
    • 🚚 Trucks carry supplies from the railhead to the front. The further the front gets ahead, the less they carry, and they burn fuel doing it.
    • 🛠️ Engineers convert track and repair stations: 🛤️ Track pushes the railhead on, Station 🚉 fixes stations first. The Soviets wreck stations as they retreat: the faster you take them, the less damage.
    • ✈️ Bombers slow Soviet resistance. Put them where air recon reports a build-up to weaken the counterattack. They burn fuel from that sector's depot.
    • 🪖 Reserves arrive at Warsaw. Send them east by train: infantry is cheap, panzers move fast but drink fuel, artillery beats counterattacks but eats ammo. Every unit you add needs feeding.
    • ⛽ / 💥 Pick each sector's fuel and ammo mix. The button underlined in green matches what it uses.
    • ⏸️ Hold when supply can't keep up: a front on a trickle barely moves and wastes what arrives. Holding digs in (much stronger defence) while stocks build; with 2 days in hand an advance goes 30% faster.
    • 🛡️ Soviet counterattacks hit the weakest sector. Keep ammo at the front, or they break through.
    • 💥 Soviet bombers hit the fullest depot. Don't pile everything in one place.
